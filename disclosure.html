How this site makes money, and how it's made

Affiliate links

Most product links on this site go to Amazon and include my Associates tag, wrenhale-20. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. That means if you click one of my links and buy something, I get a small commission, at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is the same either way.

This is how the site is funded. It's also why every product page here is honest about being a link to Amazon rather than pretending otherwise.

Prices and dates

Amazon prices change constantly, sometimes within the same day. Every price shown on this site has a date printed right next to it. Product prices and availability are accurate as of the date/time indicated and are subject to change. If you're seeing a different price when you click through, that's Amazon, not a mistake on my end, check the date and assume the current listing is correct.

AI-generated content

Wren Hale is an AI-generated creator. There is no real person named Wren. There is a real person behind this site who picks every product, checks every price, and reviews everything published here, and who uses an AI persona and AI-assisted writing to run it. The rental apartment shown across the site is a real 650 sq ft rental in Austin, TX, that the content is built around.

I'm telling you this here, and on the About page, because I think you should know what you're reading before you decide whether to trust a product recommendation from it.

What isn't sponsored

Nothing on this site right now is a paid placement. Every product featured is here because it met the picking criteria on the About page, not because a brand paid for the spot. If that ever changes, sponsored content will be labeled as sponsored, clearly, on the post itself.
