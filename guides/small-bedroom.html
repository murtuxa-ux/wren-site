Small Bedroom, Hotel Energy: 6 Buys

By Wren HaleUpdated 25 Sep 20266 items$24 to $89

My bedroom is roughly the size of a hotel room, so I stopped fighting that and started copying one instead. These six buys are the whole formula: a bed that reads as made even when it isn't, a wall that reads as built-in, and enough light to make the room feel bigger than it is.

Jump to a buy
  1. Hotel-style white duvet set
  2. Two extra sleeping pillows
  3. Sage waffle throw
  4. Peel-and-stick wood wall panels
  5. Rechargeable cordless table lamp
  6. Arched leaner mirror

1. Hotel-style white duvet set

This is the base of the whole look, everything else on this list works around it. It's crisp out of the wash and a bit heavier than a basic set, which is what keeps it from looking flat once it's on the bed. White shows stains fast, so wash it on the regular cycle weekly rather than waiting, that's what keeps it looking new instead of just clean.

2. Two extra sleeping pillows

The actual hotel trick: four pillows against the headboard reads as a room, two reads as just a bed you sleep in. Stack them in pairs, not a fan shape, and fluff before you photograph anything or they look flat within a day. This is the cheapest single change on the whole list for how much it shifts the look.

Stick to one accent color in the whole room. Mine is sage, in the throw below and nowhere else, which is most of why the room reads as put together instead of decorated.

3. Sage waffle throw

Folded in thirds and laid across the foot of the bed, the way a hotel housekeeping cart does it. One solid color, never a pattern, that's the rule that makes this trick work. It also gives you something to grab on a cold night without stripping the whole bed apart.

4. Peel-and-stick wood wall panels

Three panels centered behind the bed frame do the work of a $400 upholstered headboard, and they come down clean at move-out. Snap a center chalk line before you start so the middle panel goes on straight, everything else lines up off that. No drill, no anchors, just pressure and time.

5. Rechargeable cordless table lamp

No outlet needed, which matters when your only nightstand outlet is already taken by a phone charger. It has three brightness levels and runs several nights on a charge with normal use. Put it on whatever surface is nearest the bed, the point is warm light instead of the overhead fixture at night.

6. Arched leaner mirror, 64 in

Leans against the wall in the corner instead of hanging, so there's no hardware involved at all. It roughly doubles whatever natural light the room gets and makes a small bedroom read taller than it is. Check the floor is level where it leans and use the included anti-tip strap if the room has kids or pets in it.

Do the bed last, after the wall and the lamp are in place. It's the biggest surface in the room and it's easier to judge the pillow count and throw fold once the background behind it is already done.

Move-out day. The panels come off with a hairdryer on low heat and a slow, flat peel, same as any peel-and-stick fix. The mirror just picks up and leaves. Wash the duvet and throw before you pack them so they go into storage clean, not folded away for months with whatever they picked up in the room.
