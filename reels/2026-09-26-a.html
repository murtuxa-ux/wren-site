Questions renters ask

Does peel-and-stick tile work inside a shower?

Yes, if the sheets are rated waterproof and the wall is clean and dry before you start. Leave it 24 hours before the first shower.

Will it damage the original tile?

Not usually. Warm each sheet with a hairdryer and peel at a low angle. Clean leftover glue with rubbing alcohol.

Do I need my landlord's permission?

It is removable, but check your lease. A photo of the original tile before you start is your proof at move-out.
