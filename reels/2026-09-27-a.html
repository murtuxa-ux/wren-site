Questions renters ask

Is peel-and-stick backsplash safe behind a stove?

Keep it away from direct flame and check the heat rating on the pack. Most are fine behind an electric range with a gap.

Will it peel paint off at move-out?

It can on cheap flat paint. Warm it with a hairdryer, peel slowly at a low angle, and test a corner on day one.

Does it look real?

From normal distance, yes. Up close you can tell it is vinyl. Matching the grout lines matters more than the brand.
